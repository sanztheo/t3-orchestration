# Library policy

- Keep durable knowledge only: decisions with their reasons, contracts, procedures, pitfalls,
  incidents worth remembering, and where artifacts live. A note must move the project forward or
  prevent redoing work.
- Never record progress, turn states, or a go-ahead that only covers one task or batch.
- Every note cites a traceable source. Distinguish an explicit user decision, an agent's account,
  an assumption and a verified fact. An old go-ahead only covers its own scope.
- Never store secrets, credentials, personal or customer data, raw transcripts or audio.
- Corrections use `supersedes`; published notes and generated views are never edited by hand.
